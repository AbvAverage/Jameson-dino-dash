The adventure begins

One tap to jump. A whole jungle to beat.

Jump!
  1. 1

    Jump the toy blocks

    Tap, or press Space. The blocks are covered in the letters of Jameson's name.

  2. 2

    Double jump the mud

    Tap twice to flip over mud. It turns into sticky tar in Volcano Valley and puddles at the beach.

  3. 3

    Stay low for pterodactyls

    A warning flashes, then they swoop in. Keep your feet on the ground and they sail right over.

  4. 4

    Grab dinos and trucks

    Pop the bubbles for points. Dino +5, Truck +3.

  5. 5

    Blast with dino power

    Grab the lightning orb, then tap Blast (or press F) to smash blocks. Boom +2 a block.

  6. 6

    Find the bonus portal

    Grab 20 dinos or trucks in one run and a portal opens. Jump through for 30 seconds of bolt hunting in the sky.
